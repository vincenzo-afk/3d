# Source-Matched Broadside Notes

The reference route is intentionally near-black on a near-black background. In the rendered companion the scope/objective, stock opening, trigger highlight, receiver edge, under-action rail, barrel highlight, and muzzle edge remain detectable, but material and component IoU are expected to be weak. This is a known photo-reconstruction probe limitation rather than evidence of missing procedural geometry. Geometry review must use the neutral-light route and orbit captures; the dark route is retained to document reference presentation matching.
