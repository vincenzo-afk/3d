# Blockout Acceptance Notes

The comparison sheet shows a clear code-only sniper-rifle identity and a complete source-to-render construction correspondence: skeleton stock, bolt-action receiver, long optic, dark chassis, long barrel, compact muzzle, and under-action support. The render is intentionally neutral-lit while the source is dark and richly textured, so the sheet is used for geometry/feature critique rather than direct color matching.

Accepted blockout strengths: full object span, long shallow rifle read, stock opening, scope/bolt identity, barrel/muzzle continuity, repeated rail teeth, and no imported assets. Remaining bounded mismatches: the source has a more organic stock contour and ornate blue-teal coating, the reference objective bell is more tapered, and the bright under-action mechanism is more metallic and finely segmented. These are carried forward to form/material/surface passes rather than blocking the structural pipeline.
