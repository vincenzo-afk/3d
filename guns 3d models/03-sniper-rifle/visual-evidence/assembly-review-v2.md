# Assembly Review v2

The three-quarter view confirms that the stock, scope, receiver, barrel root, muzzle, and compact under-action spring system are now volumetric and readable. The optic has a proper tapered objective, and the stock tendrils remain attached to the front surface.

The top view exposes remaining quality issues that must be corrected before calling every part finished. The stock/ornament tubes sit slightly proud of the surface and read like loose wires from above; the receiver top rail is too tooth-heavy and visually dominates the scope base; the barrel highlight is too bright and continuous; and several small under-action/trigger pieces appear detached or floating because their z-depth and support relationships are not sufficiently grounded. The next pass should recess the tendrils, simplify the top rail teeth, shorten the barrel highlight, and add contact/bridge geometry for the spring support and trigger group.
