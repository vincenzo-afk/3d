# Material Evidence — 03 Sniper Rifle

Six source-grounded regions were analyzed from the admitted broadside image: dark anodized metal on the receiver/barrel, blue-violet/teal ornamental coating on the stock, charcoal polymer on the stock chassis, cavity/recess response, tinted optic glass, and brighter polished action metal on the bolt/under-action hardware.

The analyzer produced independent albedo, roughness, height, normal, and AO evidence maps for every region. Registry review promoted all regions to `proceed` with canonical profile IDs: `metal.steel-brushed`, `coating.painted-metal`, `plastic.matte`, `plastic.matte` for cavity response, `glass.clear`, and `metal.steel-polished`.

The near-black metal and cavity crops are low-information because the source background and subject recesses are both dark. Their profile assignments are therefore controlled approximations, not measured scans. Validation must use neutral-light, grazing-light, source-matched broadside, and three-quarter orbit views. The blue ornamental coating is the strongest visible material cue and remains the primary look-development target.
