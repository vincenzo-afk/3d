# 04 Shotgun — Material Spec Wiring

The region analysis was applied in place to the ObjectSculptSpec with probe allowance only for the explicitly documented rubber/cavity crop. Material bindings now attach to stock, receiver, upper barrel, lower magazine tube, pump, trigger, front band, and muzzle components. Each material retains independent albedo, roughness, height/normal, AO, wear, and local-override intent; no albedo map is reused as a scalar or normal channel.
