# 04 Shotgun — Blockout Review v1

The initial procedural broadside is readable as a shotgun, but it is not yet reference-quality. The stock is too thick and too short in the horizontal profile, the stock opening is too large and circular, the receiver is too tall and too dark, and the trigger guard hangs too low. The source has a longer, more organic walnut stock with a subtle cheek line and grip transition.

The pump sleeve is too short and currently reads as a ribbed rectangular block. It must extend farther forward and sit between the upper barrel and lower magazine tube with a clearly visible wood contour and end carving. The source's upper barrel and lower tubular magazine are long, thin, and separated; the blockout's tubes are present but visually crowded by the receiver/pump proportions.

The front band and muzzle are too compact and blocky. The reference has a long upper barrel, a lower tube, a narrow support band near the front, a small front sight post, and a short stepped/knurled cap at the distal end. Neutral lighting reveals these geometry issues independently of the black-on-black source presentation. The next correction pass will lengthen and slim the whole assembly, reduce receiver height, reshape the stock/pump, and improve warm wood versus blued-steel material contrast.
