# 04 Shotgun — Final Broadside and Three-Quarter Review

The final studio broadside has improved material separation and now reads as a complete pump-action shotgun: warm hardwood stock and pump, dark blued receiver and tubes, visible ejection-port recess, open trigger guard, ribbed sliding fore-end, front band, sight post, and stepped muzzle cap. The render remains intentionally procedural rather than photogrammetric, but the dominant reference parts and proportions are present.

The final three-quarter view confirms the pump slide rod, dual-tube clearance, front-band support, muzzle bore, and receiver/stock contact. Small surface details remain intentionally restrained in depth so they do not float away from the broadside shell. The model is ready for final top/side confirmation and deterministic pipeline evidence.
