# 04 Shotgun — Blockout Review v2

The second broadside is structurally closer: the stock is slimmer, the opening is no longer a large circular hole, the receiver is less oversized, and the trigger group sits nearer the receiver. The dual-tube layout, front band, front sight, and stepped muzzle are now clearly legible.

The next quality gap is material and surface hierarchy. In the studio route, the dark wood and blued steel collapse toward black; the reference has a warm brown hardwood stock and pump with visible broad grain and a more legible dark-metal receiver. In neutral mode the shape reads, but the stock still has a somewhat angular butt-to-grip transition and the pump reads as a rectangular rib block rather than a shaped sliding fore-end.

The next pass should keep the current silhouette, improve studio exposure/material floors, add explicit wood grain streak meshes and checkering/rib relief, add receiver action seams and fastener crowns, and make the barrel/magazine tube separation and muzzle cap highlights more visible. Orbit review is required before declaring the blockout gate complete.
