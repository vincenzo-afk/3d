# 04 Shotgun — Top and Side Orbit Review v4

The top orbit confirms the two long tubes are parallel and the pump sits over the lower tube with visible clearance. The front band aligns both tubes, the muzzle cap has a distinct bore ring, and the stock has nonzero thickness rather than being a flat card. The pump and receiver are still somewhat box-like from above; the next pass should taper the pump top/bottom edges and add a clearer tube guide/slide rod.

The side orbit verifies the muzzle bore and upper/lower tube separation, but the view also exposes a small floating sight/fastener cluster near the distal end and a weakly grounded underside silhouette. The trigger guard is attached, but the stock sling loop and some small support pieces need clearer parent contact and less shadow separation. These are depth/presentation corrections rather than a failure of the main shotgun construction.
