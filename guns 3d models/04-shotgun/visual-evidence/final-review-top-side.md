# 04 Shotgun — Final Top and Side Review

The final top orbit confirms the model is volumetric: the stock has thickness, the receiver has a separate shell, the upper barrel and lower magazine tube remain parallel, the pump sleeve clears the tube system, the front band bridges both tubes, and the muzzle cap/bore are separate nested parts. The added pump slide rod is visible as a continuous support under the fore-end.

The final side orbit confirms the muzzle bore and crown are recessed/grounded, the front sight and band cluster remain attached, and the trigger/guard is part of the receiver underside rather than a floating card. The end-on view is intentionally dark under neutral lighting because it looks into the blued-metal and cavity surfaces; the source-studio broadside remains the primary material evidence and the three-quarter/top routes provide the construction evidence.
