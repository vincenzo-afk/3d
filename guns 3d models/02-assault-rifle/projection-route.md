# Projection Route — 02 Assault Rifle

The admitted reference is a single broadside patterned weapon image, so the primary evaluation route is `reference-projection` with a fitted orthographic/broadside camera. This preserves the supplied blue-violet/teal coating placement for front-view comparison while the authored procedural route remains responsible for real thickness, slots, rails, fasteners, optic volume, and orbit behavior.

The camera estimate is stored in `reference-camera.json`. `reference-delit-albedo.png` and `reference-delit-report.json` are reference-derived approximations only; they are not treated as exact inverse-rendered albedo. Hidden receiver, stock, handguard, and optic geometry remain inference-level evidence.
