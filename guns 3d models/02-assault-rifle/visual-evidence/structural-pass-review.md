# Structural Pass Review

The structural pass is a code-only refinement over the completed blockout factory. The runtime hierarchy exposes seven named macro groups, 134 named meshes, three interaction sockets, and part-level metadata. Receiver upper/lower shells, stock buffer tube and brace rails, optic mount, handguard shell and rail teeth, magazine body/ribs, foregrip body/grooves, barrel step, and muzzle slots remain separate procedural pieces. Existing broadside, neutral, three-quarter, top, and side captures are reused as the fixed review set; no imported mesh or network asset is introduced.

The structural review focuses on contact continuity and non-floating attachments. Remaining single-view uncertainty is limited to hidden rear surfaces; the three-quarter and side renders show a non-degenerate depth envelope.
