# Heavy Machine Gun Multi-Angle Review — v1

The neutral orbit captures prove that the procedural model is not a flat image substitute. The three-quarter view shows receiver thickness, the hanging ammunition box depth, barrel concentricity, carry-handle arch, handguard shell, and deployed bipod attachment. The top view exposes the rail-to-handguard relationship, the rounded receiver crown, the stock thickness, and the muzzle collar stack. The side view shows a real muzzle bore/crown, front sight tower, feed-box edge, and two separate bipod legs. The rear view shows a closed rear stock volume, receiver depth, carry-handle mounts, and a continuous barrel/muzzle assembly.

The orbit review also identifies defects that a broadside silhouette gate cannot see. The stock and handguard shells are too slab-like in depth and lack the source's rounded/organic side-wall transitions. The forward handguard vents and bipod perforations are present but simplified. The neutral route is over-lifted: major painted-composite panels read pale blue rather than the source's dark coated substrate, so material comparison must use the source-studio render separately from neutral geometry inspection. The top view shows the rail teeth as dense vertical repetition, but the receiver-to-handguard transition and barrel support should receive additional collars and bevels. The side view confirms attachment continuity and is not degenerate.

Action: refine-code. Keep the current factory and sockets; refine material balance in the neutral route and add/strengthen rounded shell bevels, rear stock depth, handguard front/rear collars, and bipod leg perforation contrast without changing the high-level assembly contract.
