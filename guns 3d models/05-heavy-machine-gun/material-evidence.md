# Heavy Machine Gun Material Evidence

The admitted broadside source supports usable reference-derived pixel evidence for dark blued steel, painted composite panels, matte polymer grip surfaces, brass cartridges, dark belt links, bipod steel, machined muzzle steel, cavity blackness, and the small sight inset. `material-analysis.json` records independent albedo, roughness, height, normal, and ambient-occlusion outputs per crop with source provenance and confidence.

The material route is not exact photogrammetric recovery. Single-image inverse rendering cannot uniquely recover physical PBR, and the stylized source includes baked lighting. The patterned blue/teal/violet coating is therefore kept as a procedural painted-metal approximation with clearcoat and independent height/roughness response. The cavity region remains a deliberate `probe` because the source does not expose its substrate. Neutral-light and grazing-angle render review are required before claiming material fidelity.
