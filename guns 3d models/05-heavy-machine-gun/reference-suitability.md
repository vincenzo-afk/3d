# Reference Suitability — 05 Heavy Machine Gun

## Verdict

**PASS with single-view limitations.** The supplied 1672×941 PNG is decodable, large enough for silhouette and material-region analysis, and contains one connected firearm subject against a clean near-black background. The broadside presentation exposes the complete longitudinal silhouette, major attachment relationships, exposed feed belt, ammunition box, handguard, muzzle, stock, grip, carry handle, and deployed bipod.

The image is suitable as the primary broadside ground truth for macro proportions, visible component placement, silhouette, color grouping, and the decorative coating approximation. It is not sufficient for exact hidden-side geometry, internal action topology, true caliber, bore depth, or unoccluded rear/top/underside details. Those items remain inferred and are recorded in `image-analysis.md` and the pre-spec assessment.

## Known evidence limits

The source is a single stylized render or product-style image rather than a calibrated multi-view capture. The dark background improves the subject mask but lowers confidence for black-on-black cavity boundaries. The patterned blue/teal surface is visible only on the near side, so a procedural mirrored/continued approximation is preferred over claiming an exact full-surface texture projection.
